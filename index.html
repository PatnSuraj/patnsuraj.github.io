About

Full-stack, cloud and data, with people in the loop

I have more than three years of professional experience across back-end development, cloud infrastructure and front-end engineering in Python, Java, JavaScript and TypeScript. At Corkum LABS, in Dalhousie's Department of Psychology and Neuroscience, I led delivery of a bilingual e-health platform for clinical sleep research, from the first wireframe to the App Store.

Most of my work sits between technical systems and non-technical people. I show a wireframe before writing code, demo progress every week, and write documentation a researcher can follow without me in the room. I pair that with hands-on AWS serverless work, .NET and Azure microservices, and machine learning, backed by three AWS certifications.
